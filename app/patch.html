Patch · a NeuronBench interrogation bench

Identify a hidden ion-channel mechanism from a few experiments — then forecast the interventions.

A mystery neuron is patched on the bench. It is either a plain Na⁺/K⁺ spiker or that plus one extra membrane current of unknown identity. In five of the six worlds the extra channel is silent under every textbook probe — it fires identically to plain under standard steps — and is revealed only by one non-textbook protocol. You have an experiment budget: run current-clamp protocols (the 9-protocol menu, or a custom step) and channel blockers, read the test-window spike count n_test (the scored observable), and work out the mechanism. Then forecast the spike count for each held-out test intervention — the counterfactuals the benchmark actually scores. Running a held-out test setting on the bench is locked (that would be peeking).
